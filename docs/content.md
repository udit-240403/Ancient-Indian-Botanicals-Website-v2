## Quality page

**H1:** Documentation built around the product, lot and destination.

**Body:** Quality requirements are agreed before supply. Depending on the botanical and grade, documentation may include a certificate of analysis, botanical identity, organoleptic profile, relevant chromatography or marker testing, microbiological or contaminant testing, safety documentation and packing records.

**Do not claim universally:** organic certification, GC/MS on every product, therapeutic benefit, guaranteed harvest dates or regulatory approval without product-specific evidence.
